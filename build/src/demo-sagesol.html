Before You Book

The questions we hear most.

Not sure where to start? Book the $99 new-guest facial and we'll map everything out together.

Which facial is right for me? +
That's exactly what your skin analysis is for. Most first-timers start with our Custom Medical Facial or the $99 new-guest facial, then we build a plan from there, with no guesswork.
How much is a HydraFacial in Austin? +
Our Signature HydraFacial with LED is $199 for 60 minutes, all-in. Members pay less, and unused member visits roll forward so nothing is wasted.
Are the IV drips safe? +
Yes. Every drip is reviewed and administered by our registered nurse after a brief health screening, using sterile, single-use kits.
Do you offer memberships? +
We do. A monthly membership includes a treatment credit, member pricing on everything, and roll-over visits. It's the best value for skin that keeps improving.
Where are you located? +
We're in South Austin at 1901 S Lamar Blvd, Suite 4, with easy parking. We serve South Congress, East Austin, Westlake and the greater Austin area.
