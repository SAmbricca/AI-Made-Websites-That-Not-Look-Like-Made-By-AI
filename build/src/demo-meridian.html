Questions

Everything you want to know first.

Book a free consult and we'll answer the rest in person, with a written plan and honest pricing.

How much does laser hair removal cost in Denver? +
Single sessions start at $99, but most guests save with a package, priced at your free consult based on the treatment area. We offer 0% financing to spread it out.
Do I qualify for the GLP-1 weight-loss program? +
Most adults with a qualifying BMI or weight-related condition do. A physician reviews your health history and labs first, because safety comes before any prescription.
Is there downtime with body contouring? +
CoolSculpting® Elite has essentially no downtime, so most guests return to work the same day. Morpheus8 and resurfacing have a few days of redness we'll walk you through.
Are your providers medically supervised? +
Yes. Meridian is led by a board-certified medical director, and every treatment plan and prescription is physician-reviewed.
Where are you located? +
We're in Cherry Creek at 250 Detroit St, Suite 300, Denver. We serve Cherry Creek, Wash Park, LoDo and the greater Denver metro.
