Good to Know

Questions we're asked in every consult.

Still unsure? Bring your questions to a complimentary consult, with no obligation to treat.

Will Botox make me look frozen? +
No. Our entire philosophy is natural movement. We dose conservatively and bring you back for a complimentary two-week touch-up so we can refine rather than overdo.
How much does Botox cost in Scottsdale? +
At Lumière, Botox and Dysport are $12 per unit with transparent pricing, and a typical forehead-and-frown treatment runs 20 to 40 units. Your exact plan is quoted at your complimentary consult before anything begins.
Is there downtime after filler? +
Most guests return to work the same day. You may have minor swelling or bruising for 24 to 48 hours, which we minimize with careful technique and aftercare.
Do you offer financing? +
Yes, we offer Cherry financing with 0% options for qualifying guests, plus Allē and ASPIRE rewards to make treatments more affordable over time.
Where are you located? +
We're in Old Town Scottsdale at 7135 E Camelback Rd, Suite 210, with free covered parking. We serve Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Tempe and the greater Phoenix area.
