How I Build

A system that gets faster with every site.

The brief asked for exactly this: turn one great site into repeatable templates, so each build is quicker than the last and the whole department compounds. Here's how the system runs.

1

Design tokens, not one-offs

Every site is a shared component system: header, hero, offer, services, reviews, FAQ, booking. New client = new palette, type and copy over a proven skeleton.

2

AI drafts, human finishes

AI accelerates layout and first-draft copy; I art-direct and rewrite every line so the result is premium, on-brand, and free of the AI tells.

3

SEO & GEO baked in

Schema, local keywords, NAP and FAQ structure ship with the template, not bolted on later. Every build is search-ready on day one.

4

Client-editable handoff

Content lives in clearly marked, swappable slots for copy, photos, offers and hours, so clients update their own site without breaking the design.

5

Go High Level-ready

Booking forms and calendars are wired to drop straight into your GHL workflows, so every lead lands in the system you already run on.

6

Multi-tenant management

One template, many clients, one place to manage them. Ship an update to the system and it lifts every site you've built.

Site #1 takes a week. Site #10 takes a day. The system is the product.

Baked Into Every Build

Found by Google, cited by AI search.

Med spas live and die on local discovery. Every site I build ships with the local-SEO and GEO fundamentals in place, so you rank in the map pack and get quoted by AI assistants.

Structured data (JSON-LD)

Valid MedicalBusiness, AggregateRating, OpeningHours and OfferCatalog schema on every page: the machine-readable layer that earns rich results.

Google Business Profile alignment

Consistent NAP (name, address, phone), local keywords and location signals across the site, so your GBP and website reinforce each other.

GEO & AI-search citability

Clear FAQPage schema and question-shaped headings make each site easy for ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI to quote when locals ask "best med spa near me."

Review velocity & trust signals

Ratings, testimonials and credibility markers placed where they convert, and mirrored in schema so your star ratings can surface in search.

Core Web Vitals & speed

Self-contained, image-light builds with fonts and assets inlined for fast first paint, no layout shift, no third-party bloat slowing the booking flow.

Mobile-first & accessible

Most med spa traffic is on a phone. Every build is thumb-friendly, keyboard-navigable and readable in sunlight, because a missed tap is a missed booking.
